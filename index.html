Introduction To Hot Dish And The History Behind It

It's considerd and "poor mans or farmers food" in other words an cheap and easy to make and feed the whole family.

Ingredients Used To Make It

  • 1 pound of ground beef
  • 1 (10.5 ounce) can of cream of mushroom soup
  • Salt and ground black pepper
  • 1 (16 ounce) of frozen package tater tots
  • 2 cups of shredded cheese (pick any cheese of your choosen)

Cooking Intructions On How To Make The Hot Dish (Tater Tot Casserole)

  1. Gather all the ingredients and Preheat the oven the 350 degrees F
  2. Take a lage skillet or a pan and cook and stir the ground beef untill compltely brown, about 7 to 10 minutes. Add salt and ground pepper to the mix. (Optional: if you want you can season the ground beef for extra flavor)
  3. Once done transfer the cooked ground beef to a 9x13 baking dish. Layer the frozen tater tots on top of the ground beef. Then add the cheese on top.
  4. Now put your dish into the preheated oven for about 30 to 45 minutes. Bake untill the tater tots are golden brown.

  5. NOW ENJOY YOUR MEAL
